18 places, classes and services for kids in Rockbank, grouped below into playgrounds and parks, childcare and kindergarten, dance and music, sport clubs, and community venues. Every listing links to its official source and shows the date we last checked it. Mobile services that travel across the west are on the parties and search pages.

Rockbank is an old township turned new-estate suburb on Melbourne’s western fringe, where a historic main street and railway station now sit beside the fast-growing Woodlea estate and its new parks. Families here have the station and Rockbank Primary School at the centre of local life, and the suburb’s own list is now much stronger than the single kindergarten it once showed: six playgrounds and reserves, four early learning and outside-school-hours services plus the kindergarten, a Bharatanatyam dance school and a music studio, two sports clubs at Ian Cowie Recreation Reserve, and three community venues for hire. There is still no playgroup, library or swim school in Rockbank itself, and some of the best-regarded playgrounds in the west — Frontier Park and the Woodlea adventure playgrounds — are just over the boundary in Aintree, a short drive or bike ride away. Check the source link on each listing before you go, as session times and prices change, and expect this page to keep growing as new venues open.

Note: Rockbank Hall, Rockbank Family Services Centre and Honeydew Way Reserve are listed here with street-only addresses, as published — no street number is given for them on their sources.
