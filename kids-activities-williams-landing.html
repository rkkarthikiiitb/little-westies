Kids activities in Williams Landing

19 places, classes and services for kids in Williams Landing, grouped below into playgrounds and parks, library and library programs, kindergartens and childcare, sport clubs and coaching, and tutoring and learning. Every listing links to its official source and shows the date we last checked it. Mobile services that travel across the west are on the parties and search pages.

Williams Landing is a newer suburb built around its train station, with young families and a town centre still filling in. Little Westies now lists 19 verified kids' activities inside Williams Landing itself: four parks and playgrounds, the Library Lounge and its baby, rhyme and story sessions, four early learning centres, four sports clubs and coaching options, and three tutoring and learning studios, all checked against their own sources on 22 November 2026. Families can play for free at the reserve and the aviation-themed Ashcroft playground, join free library sessions on Overton Road, and play soccer, cricket or tennis at the reserve — but the suburb still has no swim school, dance school or community centre of its own, and neighbouring suburbs' activities are not listed here. Three parks (Ashcroft, Elmstead and Addison) are confirmed on the estate developer's pages and Visit Werribee rather than individual council pages, since Wyndham publishes its own venue page only for the Reserve and the Library Lounge.

Playgrounds and parks (4)

Williams Landing Reserve

Williams Landing

Large multi-sport reserve managed by Wyndham City Council. Playground, BBQ facilities, public toilet, basketball half court, outdoor exercise equipment, 2 ovals, 2 turf soccer pitches, 6 tennis courts (2 free public courts via Book-a-Court), 4-lane cricket nets, and a pavilion with a community room (about 50 people). Home ground of Williams Landing Sports Club (cricket, summer), Williams Landing Football Club (soccer, winter) and Williams Landing Tennis Club (year-round). A dog off-leash area sits within the reserve — it is a dog park, not a children's playground.

Address
44 Williams Landing Boulevard, Williams Landing VIC 3027
Ages
All ages
Price
Free (casual use of playground, BBQ, half court; pavilion hire fees not published)

Ashcroft Park Playground

Williams Landing

Aviation-themed playground inspired by the nearby RAAF base: a tall “control tower” with a high curved tunnel slide, dual wave slides, rope ladder and climbing wall, pyramid rope climbing frame, large sandpit with mechanical diggers, stepping stones, log balance beams, spinners, chain bridge and springers. Also a large shelter with picnic tables, BBQs, water tap, full-sized basketball court, outdoor exercise equipment and grassed area. No toilets on site (per visitor reports).

Address
36 Mandrel Drive, Williams Landing VIC 3027 (corner of Australis Drive and Mandrel Drive)
Price
Free
