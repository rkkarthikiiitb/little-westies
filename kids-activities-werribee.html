Kids activities in Werribee

In Werribee, kids can go on safari at Werribee Open Range Zoo (open daily 9am to 5pm, last entry 3:30pm) or bounce at BouncyRoos, an indoor inflatable arena for ages 3 to 12 at 13 Riverside Ave ($16.99 per 2-hour weekend session). For weekly classes there's PerformX Academy (singing, acting and dance, ages 4 to 15), Little Kickers soccer (18 months to the 8th birthday) and Kumon maths and English. Every listing was checked against its own website on 31 October 2026.

Indoor kids activities in Werribee

BouncyRoos is the main indoor option: 12 inflatable attractions under one roof, open Saturday and Sunday 10am to 4pm for bounce sessions and parties. Kumon Werribee's listed hours are Monday and Thursday, 3:15pm to 7pm.

Outdoors: Werribee Open Range Zoo

The zoo is open 365 days a year, 9am to 5pm (last entry 3:30pm), and says to “Enjoy in any weather”. Check ticket prices on the zoo site before you go.

Local tip

Werribee works well as a day-out base: the zoo covers a full outdoor stretch, and BouncyRoos at Werribee Indoor Sports is the easy indoor backup when the weather turns.

At a glance

BouncyRoos is the only Werribee listing here with a published walk-in price. For classes, pick by age: Little Kickers for under-8s, PerformX for 4 to 15, Kumon for school-age maths and English.
