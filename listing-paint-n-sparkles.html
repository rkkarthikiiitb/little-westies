Paint n Sparkles

Mobile children's party entertainer offering face painting, balloon twisting, glitter tattoos, giant bubbles and art and craft parties. Face painters can complete 10-12 full faces an hour. The minimum face-painting booking is 1.5 hours, at the business's discretion depending on distance to the venue. Entertainers have Working with Children Checks and $20 million public liability insurance. Phone 0425 782 266.

What to expect

  • The entertainment page lists face painting from $240 for 1 hour (up to 12 children), $270 for 1.5 hours (up to 18), $310 for 2 hours (up to 25) and $350 for 2.5 hours (up to 30).
  • Face painting plus balloon twisting packages start at $270 for 1 hour (up to 6 children), then $300 for 1.5 hours, $350 for 2 hours and $420 for 2.5 hours.
  • The entertainment page says the team can face paint 10-12 full face designs an hour, and also offers quicker designs.
  • The FAQ says the minimum face-painting booking is 1.5 hours, at Paint n Sparkles' discretion depending on the distance to the venue (https://paintnsparkles.com.au/faq/).
  • The FAQ says Sparkly Sue travels up to 30 minutes from Keilor Downs, and that travel charges may be applicable if the venue is further than that.
  • The homepage says all entertainers and staff have working with children cards and $20 million public liability insurance (https://paintnsparkles.com.au/).
  • The contact and entertainment pages list the booking number as 0425 782 266 (https://paintnsparkles.com.au/contact/).
