Kids activities in Deanside

10 places, classes and services for kids in Deanside, grouped below into playgrounds and parks, childcare and kindergarten, and outside school hours care. Every listing links to its official source and shows the date we last checked it. Mobile services that travel across the west are on the parties and search pages.

Deanside is a new suburb on Melton’s northern edge, where the streets, school and community buildings are arriving ahead of many of the shops and services families usually rely on. For parents, that means the local offer is still mostly parks and early learning, with most classes and activities a short drive away in Caroline Springs, Taylors Hill or Melton itself. The verified list below has 10 places: four free neighbourhood parks and reserves, five early learning and kindergarten services, and before and after school care at Deanside Primary School. There is no library, playgroup, sports club or swim school based in Deanside yet, so the parks close to home do much of the work for families with young children, alongside the childcare and kindergarten services now open. Deanside Primary School sits at the heart of the suburb, and until more services arrive, families here plan around the parks, the early learning services, and the established activities in the suburbs next door.

Coming soon: Deanside Children’s and Community Centre and its kindergarten, beside Deanside Primary School, are due to open in early 2027. The centre is not listed below because it is still under construction.
