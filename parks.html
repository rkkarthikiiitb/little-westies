Playgrounds and parks in Melbourne's west, listed for families in Aintree, Burnside Heights, Caroline Springs, Cobblebank, Deanside, Deer Park, Diggers Rest, Eynesbury, Fraser Rise, Harkness, Hillside, Kurunjang, Melton, Melton South, Strathtulloh, Sunshine, Taylors Hill, Truganina, Truganina (City of Melton) and West Sunshine. These 33 parks and reserves from Melton and Brimbank councils are free to visit and range from small neighbourhood play equipment to larger reserves with sports courts, BBQs and shelters. If you are looking for playgrounds in Taylors Hill, Caroline Springs, Hillside or Melton, start here and check the council source before you go for closures or works.
