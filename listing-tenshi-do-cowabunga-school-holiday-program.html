Tenshi-Do Cowabunga School Holiday Program

Holiday program at Tenshi-Do Martial Arts, 8.30am–5.30pm, open to students, siblings and friends: karate and gymnastics drills and games, Wii sports, movies and colouring. Book 6 days and the final 2 are free (same child). Bring lunch, snacks, a named water bottle and a change of clothes. Book by messaging the form to 0402 203 567 or tenshidoma@gmail.com.
