Kids Activities in Point Cook

In Point Cook, JUMP! Swim Schools (13 Wallace Ave) teaches swimming from 3 months old for $25 per lesson, Little Kickers runs soccer for 18 months to the 8th birthday at Jamieson Way Community Sports Hall, and Kumon teaches maths and English at Point Cook Town Centre. These are the 3 Point Cook venues we've verified so far. Each was checked against its own website on 31 October 2026.
