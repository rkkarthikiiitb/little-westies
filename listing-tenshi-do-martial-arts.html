Tenshi-Do Martial Arts is a karate and ninja academy in Ravenhall, near Caroline Springs, teaching Okinawan karate and kobudo since 2009. Classes mix karate with acrobatics and gymnastics, and the dojo has its own Gymbus, a double-decker bus fitted with gymnastics equipment. Sensei Leigh-Jay: 0402 203 567, tenshidoma@gmail.com.

Facilities

  • Plenty of parent seating for the whole class
  • Drink fridge
  • Drinking water
  • Separate male and female toilets
  • Change rooms
  • Theatre room (used in the school holidays)
  • Games room (used in the school holidays)
