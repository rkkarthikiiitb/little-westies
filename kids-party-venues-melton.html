How much does a kids party cost in Melton?

Melton Waves is the cheapest listed per-child option, at $17.60 per child for its Toddler party (ages 3–6). Its 5–14 package is $22 per child, with a minimum of 10 kids. (Melton Waves)

Xplosions charges $29 per child for its Grenade package (8 and under) and $35 for Kids Karaoke or Xplosives. You need at least 8 children and a $120 deposit. (Xplosions)

Play centres price per group: Chipmunks Caroline Springs is $325 for 8 children (Chipmunks) and Landing Playcentre Ravenhall is $359 for 8 (Luxury) or $499 for 8 (Deluxe Spa Pamper), plus $4 per adult (Landing). Zebraland's Bronze, Silver and Gold packages cover 8, 10 or 12 kids, with extra children at $24.50 to $31.50 and a $100 non-refundable deposit (Zebraland).

We don't convert per-child prices into per-8-kid totals, because minimum numbers differ between venues.

Which Melton party venues are indoor?

Zebraland, Melton Waves and Xplosions are all indoor. Zebraland is an indoor play centre (Zebraland), Melton Waves parties use the wave and leisure pools (Melton Waves) and Xplosions is a bowling and karaoke venue (Xplosions).

Can I hire a hall in Melton instead?

Melton City Council lists community venues for hire and says they suit birthday parties. It asks for 10 business days' notice. (Melton City Council – venues for hire) For example, Melton Community Hall is $105 per hour for a private function before 5pm, plus insurance, cleaning and a refundable bond. (Melton Community Hall)
