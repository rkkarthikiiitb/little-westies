Rainy day kids activities in Melbourne's west

On a wet day in Melbourne's west, head indoors to Scienceworks in Spotswood (open daily 10am to 4:30pm, free for kids up to 16), play centres in Caroline Springs, Ravenhall, Melton, Sunshine North and Hoppers Crossing, Flip Out in Derrimut or BouncyRoos in Werribee. For free options, try After Schooligans (ages 5–12) at Melton and Caroline Springs libraries or Rhyme Time (ages 1–5) at Truganina Library Lounge. Every place below was checked against its own website on 31 October 2026.

Cheapest walk-in play

Of the play centres that publish entry prices, Funtastic in Caroline Springs starts lowest at $10.95 per person for all-day entry, and Zebraland in Melton is $12 with adults free. Lollipop's, Crocs and Flip Out don't show entry prices on the pages we checked.

Free rainy day options

After Schooligans (series until 8 Dec 2026) and Family Tales (until 28 Nov 2026) are free at Melton and Caroline Springs libraries. Rhyme Time is free at Truganina Library Lounge, 10:30–11am, with upcoming dates 4, 10, 11, 17 and 18 Nov 2026. Scienceworks entry is free for kids up to 16.

Good to know

Melton Waves says its wave and toddler pool is temporarily closed, and the steam room, spa and warm water pool close on Monday 2 November for maintenance (checked 31 Oct 2026).

Local tip

Best to take advantage of numerous play centres for kids with different themes — from Flip Out in Derrimut to Chipmunks and the rest.

At a glance

For the lowest cost, Scienceworks (kids free) and the library sessions are hard to beat. For a burn-off-energy morning, pick a play centre close to home and check its entry price on the source link first.

Quick answers

Where to take kids on a rainy day in Melbourne's west?

Indoor options we have checked include Scienceworks in Spotswood (open daily 10am to 4:30pm, kids up to 16 free), play centres in Caroline Springs, Ravenhall, Melton, Sunshine North and Hoppers Crossing, Flip Out Derrimut, BouncyRoos in Werribee and SuperPark at Highpoint.

What can I do with my child on a rainy day?

Pick by age. Rhyme Time at Truganina Library Lounge is free for ages 1 to 5, Funtastic in Caroline Springs has a toddler zone for under-3s, and After Schooligans is free for ages 5 to 12 at Melton and Caroline Springs libraries.

Are there free rainy day activities in Melbourne's west?

Yes. Scienceworks is free for kids up to 16 (adults $15), and After Schooligans, Family Tales and Rhyme Time library sessions are free.

Where to go in Melbourne on a rainy day for a full day out?

Scienceworks in Spotswood is open daily 10am to 4:30pm. Werribee Open Range Zoo says to “Enjoy in any weather” and is open daily 9am to 5pm (last entry 3:30pm).
