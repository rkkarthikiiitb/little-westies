This weekend in Melbourne's west: things to do with kids

Saturday 14 November – Sunday 15 November 2026. Family events across Melton, Wyndham, Brimbank and the inner west, each checked against the council, organiser or ticketing page.

Last checked: 14 November 2026. Diwali Milan tickets in Melton are sold out. The Keilor Diwali sessions are free but need registration and are capped at 30 places.

9 kids' and family events this weekend, 5 of them free, in Caroline Springs, Keilor, Melton, Point Cook, Spotswood, Tarneit, West Footscray and Williams Landing. Saturday has Family Tales in Caroline Springs, Rhyme Time in Point Cook, a Pasifika market in Tarneit, a school fair in West Footscray and a Scienceworks workshop. Sunday has a family motor show in Williams Landing and Diwali events in Keilor.

Sat 14 Nov, 10am–4pm

Annunciation, Corpus Christi & St Augustines Community Fair

Corpus Christi Primary School, 380 Geelong Rd, West Footscray

Cost
Free entry (ride wristbands $40 on the day)

A school fair with rides, stalls, music, food, raffles, AFL and soccer workshops and a visit from the SES. Entry is free. Ride wristbands are $40 on the day; early-bird pricing ended 9 November. Rides are for children from kinder to Grade 6, with a minimum age of 3 and a maximum height of 150 cm. Dogs are not permitted, except registered assistance animals.
